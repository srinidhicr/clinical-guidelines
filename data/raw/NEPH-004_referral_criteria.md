## Section 1: Overview
[clause id: NEPH-004-S1]

This document defines when a patient managed under nephrology guidelines should be escalated beyond routine primary or specialist follow-up.

## Section 2: Urgent Referral Criteria
[clause id: NEPH-004-S2]

Urgent same-day referral is warranted for: potassium at or above 6.5 mmol/L, anuria for more than 12 hours, or rapidly rising creatinine over 48 hours.

## Section 3: Routine Referral Criteria
[clause id: NEPH-004-S3]

Routine specialist referral (within 4-6 weeks) is appropriate when: eGFR decline of more than 25% over 12 months despite optimized therapy.

## Section 4: Follow-up
[clause id: NEPH-004-S4]

Following any referral, the referring clinician should receive a summary of specialist findings within 2 weeks, and the patient should return for a joint review visit.
