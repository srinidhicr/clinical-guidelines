## Section 1: Overview
[clause id: CARD-005-S1]

Patients managed long-term under cardiology guidelines commonly develop related comorbidities that require coordinated management alongside their primary condition.

## Section 2: Common Comorbidities
[clause id: CARD-005-S2]

The most frequently observed comorbidities in this population are renal impairment and early heart failure. Screening for these should occur at routine follow-up visits.

## Section 3: Management Approach
[clause id: CARD-005-S3]

Comorbidity management should be integrated into the existing follow-up schedule rather than requiring separate visits where possible, with shared decision-making around medication burden. Strength of recommendation: Conditional.

## Section 4: Monitoring
[clause id: CARD-005-S4]

Annual comprehensive review is recommended for all patients with identified comorbidities, including relevant laboratory screening specific to the comorbidity profile.
