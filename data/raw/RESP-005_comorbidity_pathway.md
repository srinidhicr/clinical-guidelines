## Section 1: Overview
[clause id: RESP-005-S1]

Patients managed long-term under respiratory medicine guidelines commonly develop related comorbidities that require coordinated management alongside their primary condition.

## Section 2: Common Comorbidities
[clause id: RESP-005-S2]

The most frequently observed comorbidities in this population are osteoporosis from long-term steroid exposure and deconditioning. Screening for these should occur at routine follow-up visits, except in the following cases where screening frequency should increase to every visit rather than annually:
- Cumulative oral steroid courses exceeding 4 per year
- Prior fragility fracture at any site
- Age 70 or older regardless of steroid exposure

## Section 3: Management Approach
[clause id: RESP-005-S3]

Comorbidity management should be integrated into the existing follow-up schedule rather than requiring separate visits where possible, with shared decision-making around medication burden. Strength of recommendation: Conditional.

## Section 4: Monitoring
[clause id: RESP-005-S4]

Annual comprehensive review is recommended for all patients with identified comorbidities, including relevant laboratory screening specific to the comorbidity profile.
