## Section 1: Overview
[clause id: CARD-003-S1]

This reference summarizes standard dosing and key contraindications for commonly used vasodilating agents in cardiology.

## Section 2: Dosing Table
[clause id: CARD-003-S2]

| Drug | Standard Dose Range | Primary Contraindication |
|------|---------------------|---------------------------|
| Cardaprilol | 5-10 mg once daily | severe renal artery narrowing |
| Rhythmostat | 25-50 mg twice daily | second-degree conduction block |
| Flowtensin | 2.5 mg once daily, titrate to 10 mg | pregnancy |

Flowtensin should not be titrated beyond 10 mg once daily under any circumstance; doses above this threshold have shown no additional benefit in the reference population and increase orthostatic hypotension risk.

**Renal dose adjustment (Cardaprilol only):**

| eGFR (mL/min/1.73m2) | Adjusted Dose |
|---|---|
| ≥ 45 | 5-10 mg once daily (standard) |
| 30-44 | 2.5-5 mg once daily |
| < 30 | Do not initiate; select alternative per Section 3 |

## Section 3: Contraindications
[clause id: CARD-003-S3]

Each agent listed above should be avoided in the presence of its primary contraindication. Cardaprilol additionally requires caution in patients with a history of hypersensitivity reactions to related compounds.

## Section 4: Drug Interactions
[clause id: CARD-003-S4]

Concurrent use of more than one agent from this table requires dose adjustment and increased monitoring frequency, particularly in patients over 65 or with reduced organ clearance.
