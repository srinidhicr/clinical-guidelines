## Section 1: Overview
[clause id: RESP-003-S1]

This reference summarizes standard dosing and key contraindications for commonly used bronchodilating agents in respiratory medicine.

## Section 2: Dosing Table
[clause id: RESP-003-S2]

| Drug | Standard Dose Range | Primary Contraindication |
|------|---------------------|---------------------------|
| Bronchaline | 2 puffs (90 mcg) every 6 hours as needed | known hypersensitivity to propellant |
| Airoflux | 250 mcg twice daily via inhaler | untreated systemic fungal infection |
| Respiguard | 40 mg once daily | active peptic ulcer disease |

## Section 3: Contraindications
[clause id: RESP-003-S3]

Each agent listed above should be avoided in the presence of its primary contraindication. Bronchaline additionally requires caution in patients with a history of hypersensitivity reactions to related compounds.

## Section 4: Drug Interactions
[clause id: RESP-003-S4]

Concurrent use of more than one agent from this table requires dose adjustment and increased monitoring frequency, particularly in patients over 65 or with reduced organ clearance.
