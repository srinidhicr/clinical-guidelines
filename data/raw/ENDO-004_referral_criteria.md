## Section 1: Overview
[clause id: ENDO-004-S1]

This document defines when a patient managed under endocrinology guidelines should be escalated beyond routine primary or specialist follow-up.

## Section 2: Urgent Referral Criteria
[clause id: ENDO-004-S2]

Urgent same-day referral is warranted for: blood glucose sustained above 350 mg/dL with ketosis, or a new thyroid storm presentation (fever, tachycardia, agitation). Note the distinction: blood glucose above 350 mg/dL *without* ketosis does not on its own meet urgent criteria — it should be treated as routine follow-up with a repeat reading within 24-48 hours unless other urgent features are present.

## Section 3: Routine Referral Criteria
[clause id: ENDO-004-S3]

Routine specialist referral (within 4-6 weeks) is appropriate when: HbA1c remaining above target after two consecutive quarterly reviews despite therapy adjustment.

## Section 4: Follow-up
[clause id: ENDO-004-S4]

Following any referral, the referring clinician should receive a summary of specialist findings within 2 weeks, and the patient should return for a joint review visit.
