## Section 1: Overview
[clause id: NEPH-001-S1]

Progressive Renal Filtration Decline Syndrome (PRFDS) is a synthetic condition used for this guideline set. Patients typically present with symptoms managed through a stepwise approach involving lifestyle modification followed by pharmacologic therapy with renal-protective agents.

## Section 2: First-Line Management
[clause id: NEPH-001-S2]

First-line management of PRFDS consists of initiating Nephrostat at 2.5 mg once daily, adjust for eGFR, alongside structured lifestyle counseling. Strength of recommendation: Strong.

**Do not:**
- Initiate Nephrostat in patients with confirmed bilateral renal artery stenosis (absolute contraindication, see dosing reference)
- Increase the dose within the first 2 weeks regardless of response — early titration is associated with acute kidney injury in the reference population

## Section 3: Second-Line Management
[clause id: NEPH-001-S3]

If first-line therapy does not achieve target control within 8-12 weeks, add Kalibind at 15 g once daily with food. Strength of recommendation: Conditional.

## Section 4: Monitoring
[clause id: NEPH-001-S4]

Patients on therapy for PRFDS should be reviewed every 3 months for the first year, then every 6 months once stable, with routine labs to screen for treatment-related adverse effects.
