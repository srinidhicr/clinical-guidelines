## Section 1: Overview

This reference summarizes standard dosing and key contraindications for commonly used anti-infective agents in infectious disease.

## Section 3: Contraindications
[clause id: ID-003-S3]

Each agent listed above should be avoided in the presence of its primary contraindication. Bactrizole additionally requires caution in patients with a history of hypersensitivity reactions to related compounds. Bactrizole should not be combined with Infectiguard-X despite both being oral agents with non-overlapping primary contraindications — the combination is specifically flagged in Section 4 due to additive QT prolongation risk, which is not captured by either drug's individual contraindication alone.

## Section 4: Drug Interactions
[clause id: ID-003-S4]

Concurrent use of more than one agent from this table requires dose adjustment and increased monitoring frequency, particularly in patients over 65 or with reduced organ clearance. Bactrizole and Infectiguard-X specifically should not be prescribed together: both agents independently carry a mild QT-prolonging effect that is additive in combination, even though neither drug lists the other as a primary contraindication. If both indications are present, complete a full course of one agent before starting the other, with an ECG check prior to switching.
