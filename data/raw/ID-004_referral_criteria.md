## Section 1: Overview
[clause id: ID-004-S1]

This document defines when a patient managed under infectious disease guidelines should be escalated beyond routine primary or specialist follow-up.

## Section 2: Urgent Referral Criteria
[clause id: ID-004-S2]

Urgent same-day referral is warranted for: fever above 39.5°C with hypotension, spreading skin necrosis, or altered mental status.

## Section 3: Routine Referral Criteria
[clause id: ID-004-S3]

Routine specialist referral (within 4-6 weeks) is appropriate when: recurrence of the same infection site more than twice within 6 months. A single recurrence, or two recurrences spaced more than 6 months apart, does not meet this threshold and should be managed in primary care with standard first-line therapy repeated.

## Section 4: Follow-up
[clause id: ID-004-S4]

Following any referral, the referring clinician should receive a summary of specialist findings within 2 weeks, and the patient should return for a joint review visit.
