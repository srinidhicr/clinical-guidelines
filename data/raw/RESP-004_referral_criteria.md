## Section 1: Overview
[clause id: RESP-004-S1]

This document defines when a patient managed under respiratory medicine guidelines should be escalated beyond routine primary or specialist follow-up.

## Section 2: Urgent Referral Criteria
[clause id: RESP-004-S2]

Urgent same-day referral is warranted for: oxygen saturation below 90% on room air, inability to complete a full sentence due to breathlessness, or use of accessory muscles at rest.

## Section 3: Routine Referral Criteria
[clause id: RESP-004-S3]

Routine specialist referral (within 4-6 weeks) is appropriate when: more than two exacerbations requiring oral steroids within a 12-month period.

## Section 4: Follow-up
[clause id: RESP-004-S4]

Following any referral, the referring clinician should receive a summary of specialist findings within 2 weeks, and the patient should return for a joint review visit.
