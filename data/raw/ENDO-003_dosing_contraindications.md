## Section 3: Contraindications
[clause id: ENDO-003-S3]

Each agent listed above should be avoided in the presence of its primary contraindication. Glucostabin additionally requires caution in patients with a history of hypersensitivity reactions to related compounds.

## Section 4: Drug Interactions
[clause id: ENDO-003-S4]

Concurrent use of more than one agent from this table requires dose adjustment and increased monitoring frequency, particularly in patients over 65 or with reduced organ clearance.
