## Section 1: Overview
[clause id: RHEU-003-S1]

This reference summarizes standard dosing and key contraindications for commonly used immune-modulating agents in rheumatology.

## Section 2: Dosing Table
[clause id: RHEU-003-S2]

| Drug | Standard Dose Range | Primary Contraindication |
|------|---------------------|---------------------------|
| Immunoflex | 10-25 mg once weekly (see hepatic adjustment below) | active untreated tuberculosis |
| Jointral | 200 mg twice daily | pre-existing retinal disease |
| Autostem | 5 mg twice daily | active severe infection |

**Hepatic dose adjustment (Immunoflex only):** patients with mildly elevated liver enzymes (up to 2x upper limit of normal) may continue at the standard dose with monthly monitoring. Above 2x upper limit of normal, the dose should not simply be reduced — therapy should be held entirely until enzymes normalize, then restarted at the lowest dose (10 mg).

## Section 3: Contraindications
[clause id: RHEU-003-S3]

Each agent listed above should be avoided in the presence of its primary contraindication. Immunoflex additionally requires caution in patients with a history of hypersensitivity reactions to related compounds.

## Section 4: Drug Interactions
[clause id: RHEU-003-S4]

Concurrent use of more than one agent from this table requires dose adjustment and increased monitoring frequency, particularly in patients over 65 or with reduced organ clearance.
