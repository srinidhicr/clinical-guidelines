## Section 2: First-Line Management
[clause id: ENDO-002-S2]

Initial management of THID involves Insulyx at 0.2-0.4 units/kg/day, individually titrated. Strength of recommendation: Strong. This applies to adults under 65 with no cardiac history. In patients aged 65 or older, or with any history of arrhythmia, start at the lower end of the range (0.2 units/kg/day) and titrate no faster than every 4-6 weeks rather than at the standard pace.
