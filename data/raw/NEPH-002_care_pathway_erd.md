## Section 2: First-Line Management

Initial management of ERD involves Filtrexan at 5-10 mg once daily. Strength of recommendation: Strong. This applies when serum potassium is between 5.1 and 6.4 mmol/L. At or above 6.5 mmol/L, this is an urgent presentation per the referral criteria document and initial management should not proceed outpatient — Filtrexan initiation in that range should only begin after acute management in an urgent care setting.
