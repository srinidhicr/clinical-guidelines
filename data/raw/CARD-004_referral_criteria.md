## Section 1: Overview
[clause id: CARD-004-S1]

This document defines when a patient managed under cardiology guidelines should be escalated beyond routine primary or specialist follow-up.

## Section 2: Urgent Referral Criteria
[clause id: CARD-004-S2]

Urgent same-day referral is warranted for: sustained resting heart rate above 150 bpm, chest pain with exertion lasting more than 10 minutes, or syncope within the past 48 hours.

## Section 3: Routine Referral Criteria
[clause id: CARD-004-S3]

Routine specialist referral (within 4-6 weeks) is appropriate when: blood pressure remaining above target after 3 consecutive monthly readings on maximum tolerated therapy.

## Section 4: Follow-up
[clause id: CARD-004-S4]

Following any referral, the referring clinician should receive a summary of specialist findings within 2 weeks, and the patient should return for a joint review visit.
